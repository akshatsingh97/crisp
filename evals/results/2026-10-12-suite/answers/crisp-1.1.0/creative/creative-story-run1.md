Beep the robot wanted to bake bread, so each night it mixed flour, water, and yeast, but its loaves came out flat as stones. One evening, an old baker taught Beep to wait patiently while the dough slowly rose, and Beep learned that good things need time. By morning, a warm golden loaf sat on the table, and Beep, smiling with its little blue lights, fell asleep dreaming of the next one.
