Bolt the robot had never made anything softer than a bolt, until one rainy night when Grandma Rosa showed it how to knead dough with gentle hands. Bolt's first loaf came out flat as a coin, and its second came out hard as a rock, but Bolt kept trying, listening to the oven hum its slow, warm song. By the third night the bread rose golden and round, and Bolt shared it with Grandma Rosa, discovering that the best thing it had ever made was a little bit of warmth for someone else.
