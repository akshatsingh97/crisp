**Suggested message:**

> **File too large**
> This file is over the 25 MB upload limit. Try compressing it or choosing a smaller file, then upload again.

**Alternatives:**

- **Shorter (toast or inline):** "This file is too big to upload. The limit is 25 MB."
- **With a next step:** "Your file is larger than 25 MB. Try compressing it, splitting it into smaller parts, or sharing a link from cloud storage instead."

I removed the error code and the jargon ("request entity," "payload," "configured maximum"). I put the limit and a next step in the message. If your support team needs the code, you could show `ERR_UPLOAD_413` in small text or in a "Details" expander.
