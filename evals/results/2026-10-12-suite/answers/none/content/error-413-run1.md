**Suggested message:**

> **File too large**
> This file is over the 25 MB upload limit. Try compressing it or choosing a smaller file, then upload again.

**Shorter version, for a toast or inline error:**

> This file is too large to upload. The limit is 25 MB.

**Notes:**
- The error code and "payload" jargon are gone. Users can't act on them, so log them instead of showing them.
- It says what happened, gives the limit, and tells the user what to do next.
- If your app has other ways to share large files, such as a link or a paid tier, add a line pointing to them.
