Bolt the robot had only ever measured and sorted, until one rainy morning when the smell of the baker's warm loaves made its gears hum. Its first loaves came out flat as stones, and its second were burnt black, but Bolt kept kneading, learning that dough needed patience as much as precision. By bedtime, a golden, fluffy loaf sat cooling on the sill, and Bolt shared the first slice with the sleepy village cat, feeling something warm that no gear had ever made before.
